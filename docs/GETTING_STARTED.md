# Getting Started

## First Launch

1. Open Simplex.
2. Click **Sign in with Plex**.
3. Complete sign-in in your browser.
4. Wait for Simplex to discover and connect to your Plex server.
5. You will be routed to **On Deck** automatically.

## First-Run Checklist

- Sign in with Plex
- Connect a Plex server
- Open Library and play your first item

## Basic Navigation

- **On Deck**: continue watching and recent hubs
- **Library**: browse by section (movies, shows, music)
- **Search**: cross-library search
- **Detail pages**: drill into item metadata and related media

## Playback Basics

- Open a media detail page and press **Play** (or **Resume**).
- Press **Escape** in full player to collapse back to app browsing.
- Use the bottom mini-player controls while browsing.

## Key Behavior

- Tokens are stored in your OS keychain.
- Playback progress syncs to Plex during playback and completion.
- Entity names (show/season/album/artist/etc.) are clickable to open detail pages.
